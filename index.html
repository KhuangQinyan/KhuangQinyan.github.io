Sound Factory Records · West Hollywood

An operating system for a record label

I designed and built, from scratch, the Airtable base the whole label team works in. It connects releases, artist partnerships, budgets, studio sessions and campaign data, so one expense entry shows up in an artist's spend, a release's ROI and the budget-health view.

Role
Operations & Workflow Coordinator, promoted from Marketing Intern
Period
Jan 2026 – present
Used by
The full label team, about 8 people
Built with
Airtable, Python, Claude API, Airtable Automations
  • Six linked tables: Artists, Releases, Sessions, Contacts, Budget Line Items and Budgets. Rollups carry each expense into spend per artist, actual spend per release, and budget remaining.
  • Python scripts using the Claude API, together with Airtable Automations, collect Spotify and social media numbers automatically and feed the growth charts on every artist page.
  • Each release tracks cost per stream and cost per new follower, so the team can compare campaign spend release by release.
  • A task layer assigns release work to specific people, and everyone has a personal "My Tasks" view sorted by due date.
  • I also build the label's paid social audiences on Meta, Google and TikTok, including lookalike audiences.

Nice Life Publishing · Los Angeles

A scoring model for A&R scouting

To make artist scouting repeatable, I built a tracking database of 50+ prospective signees and a weighted score that ranks them on growth, audience quality and deal readiness.

Role
A&R Publishing Intern
Period
Feb – May 2025
Tools
Airtable, DISCO, Spotify for Artists, Excel
Result
Evaluation time per artist cut from 3–4 hours to 45–60 minutes
  • Tracked streaming data, release schedules, publishing splits and deal status for every prospect, updated weekly.
  • Built a 0 to 100 score from six weighted criteria. Above 75 is a priority target, 60 to 75 means keep watching, and below 60 is a pass unless there are exceptional circumstances.
  • In the data, listeners who found an artist through algorithmic playlists became followers at nearly twice the rate of listeners from large editorial playlists, so the model rewards organic discovery.
  • Set up alerts that flag artists who cross growth thresholds, such as more than 50% monthly growth.
